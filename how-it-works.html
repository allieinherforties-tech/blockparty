How BlockParty works

Three steps, no guesswork. Browse, book a call, and we handle the truck.

1

Browse

Look through block-party packages or pick items individually: cornhole, giant Jenga, giant/human-sized beer pong, a slushie or frozen-cocktail machine, a portable basketball hoop, or a ball pit.

2

Book a confirmation call

Every booking includes a short call with us before your event. Reach us at reserve@blockpartysupply.co to get started. We confirm your exact delivery window, where equipment sets up on your block (curb, sidewalk, or a specific spot), pickup time, and anything about your street that affects logistics — permit timing, closure hours, tight parking. Nothing gets left to guesswork.

3

We deliver and pick up

On the day, we bring everything to your block, set it up where you need it, and come back for pickup after your party. You don't drive anything, store anything, or break anything down.

Book at least 2 weeks ahead

That gives us time to confirm delivery logistics and make sure your equipment is available.
