Frequently asked

Can I choose any flavor?

The machine supports a kids' slushie flavor or an adult frozen-cocktail mix — the double-flavor unit runs two options side by side, e.g., one of each.

No — there is no self-pickup option for the machine. We deliver, set it up, and come back for pickup after your event.
