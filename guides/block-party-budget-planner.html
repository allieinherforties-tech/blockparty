Block Party Budget Planner

What a Brooklyn block party costs: real rental prices from $65/day, the $25 permit fee, the line items to price locally, and where packages beat à la carte.

Updated October 27, 2026

Here's the honest math. A block party has one small fixed city cost and one big equipment line, and everything else is food and folding chairs you price out locally. We'll give you our actual numbers — the same ones on our catalog pages — and mark clearly what we're not the source for.

The fixed city cost

The street activity permit runs $25, non-refundable even if you're denied or withdraw. If anything's amplified, there's a separate sound permit. That's the city's share of the budget — the permit fee is not where block party money goes.

Every price above includes delivery, pickup, and the pre-event confirmation call. Standalone single-item orders carry a $150 minimum order value — or a flat $75 delivery/pickup fee on items priced under $150 — and bundling with any other item clears that automatically.

And the packages earn their keep: the Classic trio books à la carte for $65 + $65 + $75 = $205, and the package is $185. Full is $420 against $460 for Classic-plus-slushie. Deluxe is $675 against $745 for Full-plus-ball-pit. Same equipment, same service, less money — that's the whole trick.

The line items we're not the source for

Food and drinks, tables and chairs, ice, cups, a canopy, trash bags, a banner for the corner — we don't rent any of it (rentals only, we don't sell equipment), so price those with local vendors. Budget honest numbers for the crowd you actually expect; the crowd-size guide keeps the equipment side from bloating.

Splitting it fairly

The budget conversation is the group chat's final boss. What works: one post with the real total, small asks split the way the block splits everything, and collection finished before the week of — not on party morning. Keep the ask modest: for most blocks, the equipment is the cheapest memorable thing on the budget.

When the numbers look right, email us — the confirmation call is where the plan gets locked.

Frequently asked

How much does a block party cost in Brooklyn?

The city's share is small — a $25 permit fee. Equipment is the main line item: BlockParty packages run $185 (Classic), $420 (Full), and $675 (Deluxe), with à la carte games from $65/day. Food, tables, and supplies are separate line items to price locally.

Does BlockParty charge for delivery?

Delivery, pickup, and the confirmation call are included with every booking. Standalone single-item orders carry a $150 minimum order value, or a flat $75 delivery/pickup fee on items priced under $150 — cleared automatically by bundling or a package.
